How It Works

Pick a route, set a price

Tell me where you are going, what you are willing to pay, and how you want to be notified. The dashboard handles the rest.

Checks every hour, all day

Every hour, the system pings Google Flights to see if prices changed. If something hits your target, you are the first to know.

Discord or email, your call

Webhook pings go straight to your Discord server. Or keep it old-school with email. Both work, neither will spam you.

See how prices move

Every check is logged. The chart shows you the trend so you know whether that $50 ticket is actually a good deal or just normal pricing.

Built for a single airport. Covers 8 now. Still free to start.

Terms of Service

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. What This Service Does

Flight Sniper checks flight prices using the SerpApi Google Flights API and notifies you when prices match your criteria. I do not sell tickets, process payments, or act as a travel agent. This is a monitoring tool, not a booking platform.

2. Price Accuracy

Prices come from Google Flights data via SerpApi. They are a snapshot, not a guarantee. Always verify the final price on the airline's website before purchasing. I have no control over pricing, availability, or airline errors.

3. Your Account

You are responsible for your account credentials. If you share your login, anything that happens under your account is on you. That said, there is no sensitive personal data stored here beyond your email and name.

4. What I Collect

Your email, name, and the routes you choose to monitor. That is it. No payment info, no location tracking, no ads. Email addresses are used only for sending price alerts you asked for.

5. Third-Party Services

This tool uses SerpApi (for flight price lookups), Discord (for webhook notifications if you choose), and standard email delivery. Each of those services has its own terms. I am not responsible for their uptime or data handling.

6. Disclaimer

Flight Sniper is provided as-is. I built it because I wanted it. It might have bugs, prices might be stale, and occasionally the API might not return results. Use it at your own discretion.

7. Changes to These Terms

If I change these terms, the updated date at the top will change. For material changes, I will send an email.

8. Contact

Open an issue on GitHub or reach out directly via the repository profile. I usually respond within a day.
